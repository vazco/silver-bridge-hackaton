# Fun Fact

Did you know that honey never spoils? Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that are over 3,000 years old and still perfectly edible! This is because honey has a unique chemical composition that makes it inhospitable to bacteria and microorganisms. Its low water content and acidic pH create an environment where very few things can survive.

The bees also add an enzyme called glucose oxidase, which produces hydrogen peroxide - a natural antibacterial compound. So basically, honey is nature's preservative!
